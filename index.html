Questions worth asking

Is The Shelf free to use?

Yes. Guests get two free recommendations to try it out — signing up is free and unlocks unlimited picks, saved history, and cover art.

Do I need an account to get a recommendation?

No. You can ask for picks right away as a guest. An account just lets you keep a running shelf of everything you've found.

How does mood matching actually work?

Pick a category and genre, then tell us how you're feeling or add a quick note — The Shelf weighs all of it to hand you a pick worth your time.

What categories does The Shelf cover?

Music, Movies & Series, Anime, Academics, Gaming, and Lifestyle — with more on the way.
